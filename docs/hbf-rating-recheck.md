Directory membership is separate from the customer satisfaction award. A missing published result is not evidence of a low rating or non-participation. HBF states that participation/publication is voluntary and that [star ratings are valid for one year](https://www.hbf.co.uk/policy/campaigns-and-initiatives/css-star-awards/css-faqs/).
